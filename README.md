## Release notes

### Upcoming

<!-- release-upcoming:start -->
<b>⚡ Improved:</b>
<ul align="left">
<li>Home screen widgets have a new look with rounder shapes. The players now show how far you are and how much time is left, and the skip buttons show your own skip lengths.</li>
<li>The Your Shows widget is now a grid of artwork. Choose whole covers or shapes in Settings › Appearance › Widgets.</li>
<li>See every boxlore widget and add it to your home screen from Settings › Appearance › Widgets.</li>
<li>On Home, a show you listen to oldest first now picks up exactly where you left off, even deep into a long series, and skips trailers. Daily mix suggests the same next episode.</li>
<li>Android Auto has a new layout. Home shows what you're midway through, then large blocks for New episodes, Your shows, Daily mix, Downloads and more, with Continue, New and Shows one tap away as tabs.</li>
<li>In Android Auto, tapping an episode in New episodes, Daily mix, Downloads, Liked or Drive picks keeps playing down that list, and an episode you resume is followed by your queue.</li>
<li>Android Auto lists now update when you queue, download, like or follow something on your phone.</li>
<li>In Android Auto, your last episode is ready on the car's screen as soon as you connect, so one tap resumes it.</li>
<li>If you installed boxlore from GitHub or another store, it now shows you the one Android Auto setting that puts it on your car's screen, the first time you use it in the car.</li>
</ul>
<b>🐛 Fixed:</b>
<ul align="left">
<li>Widgets that follow your phone's light and dark mode now switch with it, even when boxlore isn't open.</li>
<li>The Your Shows and New Episodes widgets now count your whole library instead of stopping at 50.</li>
<li>On Home, a show you've heard every episode of now lists its latest episodes instead of an empty list, and a show whose episodes can't load lets you try again.</li>
<li>In Android Auto, artwork loads faster, and every episode has a picture, in lists and in the queue: one with no image of its own shows its show's cover.</li>
<li>In Android Auto, tapping something that can't be played no longer stops what you're listening to.</li>
<li>In Android Auto, skipping forward or back no longer sends the list you're browsing back to the top.</li>
<li>Casting no longer closes boxlore when your queue has an episode your Cast device can't stream, such as one from a feed on your home network. Those episodes stay in your queue but are skipped on the Cast device.</li>
<li>After you listen on another device, Play on the mini player now starts the episode it shows, at the right spot, instead of an older one from your queue.</li>
</ul>

## Every feature

<a id="every-feature"></a>

### Discover

- **Search by idea.** Describe a topic and get shows and episodes about it, even when the words don’t match. Misspelled show names still work.
- **Recommendations.** Matched on boxlore’s servers from what you’ve listened to, liked or finished, then re-ranked on your phone. On Home, in Explore’s For You, and in Smart Queue.
- **Home.** Daily Mix, Because you like, rows for the time of day, and trending shows.
- **Lore.** Question cards that each lead to an episode. What you play and skip teaches your taste.
- **The boxlore brief.** An optional daily news episode with sources, in Global, India, US and UK editions.
- **A start that asks first.** Answer a few questions and get shows to begin with, or search, pick genres, or import your library.

### Listen

- **Player.** Audio and video episodes, 0.5× to 3× speed, and a sleep timer that can stop at the end of the episode.
- **Seek and skip.** Separate back and forward amounts from 5 to 120 seconds, optional seek buttons on the miniplayer, and intro and outro skipping with per-show overrides.
- **Chapters and transcripts.** Chapters and transcripts that follow playback, chapters read from show-note timestamps, and both saved with downloads.
- **Smart Queue.** Keeps something playing: the next episode, unfinished listening, new episodes, then recommendations. Or keep it to the show you’re playing.
- **Support a show.** A show’s funding links appear as a Support chip on its page.

### Library

- **Folders.** Group shows in folders from 1×1 to 3×3, or let boxlore sort them by genre. Reorder folders and shows however you like.
- **Tags and icons.** Give any show you follow your own tag and one of 31 icons. Tags become filters at the top of your shows.
- **Downloads.** Auto-downloads for the shows you choose, and Smart Downloads with episode and storage limits, Wi-Fi and charging options, and auto-delete for played episodes.
- **Alerts.** Choose which shows send new-episode alerts.
- **Any RSS feed.** Add a show by its feed link, including private links.

### Make it yours

- **Themes.** Classic, Pure, Material 3 from your wallpaper, or a custom theme from any colour.
- **Artwork colours.** Show and episode pages can match the show’s artwork, or keep your theme everywhere.
- **Layout.** Floating or classic navigation, Crisp, Soft or Round lettering, and player layout.

### Everywhere you listen

- **Android Auto.** Browse Home, Library, Downloads and Discover in the car. Playback picks up when you reconnect.
- **Google Cast.** Play on TVs and speakers, with the player in the app kept in sync.
- **Widgets.** Now Playing, playback controls, Your Shows and New Episodes.

### Sync and backup

- **Cloud sync.** Sign in with an optional account to back up your library and keep it in sync across your devices.
- **Full backup.** Export your shows, tags, folders, history, likes and settings to a file, and restore them later.
- **OPML.** Import and export your subscriptions to move between podcast apps.

Always free, with no app ads, no subscriptions and no paywalls.

## Questions people ask

<a id="questions-people-ask"></a>

<details>
<summary><b>Is boxlore free?</b></summary>
<br/>
Yes. No subscriptions, no paywalls, and no app ads. <a href="docs/SOUL.md">The soul of boxlore</a> explains why it stays that way.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Will I hear ads?</b></summary>
<br/>
Not from boxlore. Some podcasts include ads their creators put in their own episodes, and those play as part of the show.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>How do recommendations work?</b></summary>
<br/>
boxlore’s servers match episodes to what you’ve listened to recently. Your phone then fine-tunes the order for your playback habits. <a href="docs/PRIVACY.md">What we track and why</a> has the details.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Why can’t I find a show that’s exclusive to another app?</b></summary>
<br/>
boxlore plays public podcasts from the <a href="https://podcastindex.org/">Podcast Index</a> and any RSS feed you add. Platform exclusives aren’t published openly, so they aren’t here.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Can I add a private or paid feed?</b></summary>
<br/>
Yes, if the feed has its own private link. Go to <b>Settings → Library</b> and add it. Feeds that need a separate username and password aren’t supported yet, and new-episode alerts should stay off for private feeds.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Can I sync between phones?</b></summary>
<br/>
Yes. Sign in with an optional account to back up your library and keep it in sync across your devices. boxlore works fully without one.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Can I bring my shows from another app?</b></summary>
<br/>
Yes. Import a subscription file (OPML) when you start, or later from Settings. You can export your subscriptions or a full backup at any time.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Why can't I see boxlore in Android Auto?</b></summary>
<br/>
If you installed boxlore from GitHub or Obtainium, Android Auto hides it until you turn on one setting, Unknown sources. <a href="docs/ANDROID_AUTO.md">Use boxlore with Android Auto</a> shows each step. The Google Play version shows up in your car without it.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Is boxlore open source?</b></summary>
<br/>
The Android app is source-available under the <a href="LICENSE">PolyForm Strict License 1.0.0</a>: you can read the code and run it for personal, noncommercial use, but not redistribute it. The servers behind search and recommendations are private.
</details>

## Install

<a id="install"></a>

- **[Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=cx.aswin.boxlore)** is the recommended install.
- **[GitHub Releases](https://github.com/boxcreate/boxlore/releases/latest)** has the latest APK for sideloading.
- **[Obtainium](https://apps.obtainium.imranr.dev/redirect?r=obtainium://add/https://github.com/boxcreate/boxlore)** adds boxlore with automatic update tracking.

Android may ask you to allow installation from your browser or file manager when sideloading. boxlore needs Android 12 or later.

Using Android Auto with the GitHub or Obtainium version? Turn on one setting first: [Use boxlore with Android Auto](docs/ANDROID_AUTO.md).
