# The soul of boxlore

Technology was never supposed to be an obstacle course.

The founding promise of personal computing—and of Android in particular—was simple: when you bought a device, you owned it. You had the quiet freedom to choose your tools, install your software, and explore without asking a gatekeeper for permission. 

Over the past decade, that freedom has been steadily enclosed. Platforms born from open source now treat independent software with suspicion, turning personal computers into leased terminals. The apps followed suit. In the race to monetize every interaction, simple tools were hollowed out by extractive design: basic playback controls locked behind paywalls, clean interfaces cluttered with promotional noise, and surveillance algorithms quietly profiling every tap to maximize screen time.

Podcasting is one of the rare frontiers that refused to be enclosed.

Built on decentralized RSS and preserved by independent communities like the [Podcast Index](https://podcastindex.org/), podcasting has no single landlord. No corporation owns the protocol, and no company gets to decide what you are allowed to hear. When corporate giants poured billions into exclusive studios and algorithmic walled gardens, the open standard held its ground. Audiences refused to be captive, creators saw their reach shrink, and exclusive shows quietly returned to open feeds. It was a decisive reminder that open protocols can outlast corporate land grabs.

We built **boxlore** to be an app worthy of that openness.

We believe a podcast player should be a quiet, thoughtful companion—not an attention merchant.

- **No ads, no subscriptions, no paywalls.** You will never be asked to pay for playback speed, a sleep timer, queue management, or anything that belongs to the core listening experience. We fund boxlore out of our own day-job savings. If server costs ever outgrow what we can personally sustain, the most you will ever see is an optional tip button—never an ad, and never a feature held hostage.
- **Personalized for discovery, not addiction.** We use server-side semantic matching to understand topics and connect you with shows that genuinely match your curiosity. But our algorithms serve your interests, not advertisers. We don't sell your data, build ad profiles, or manipulate what you see to keep you hooked to a screen.
- **Rooted in an open ecosystem.** We believe in open RSS, independent directories, and the fundamental right to [keep Android open](https://keepandroidopen.org/).

None of this exists in isolation. boxlore stands on the shoulders of the open-source craftspeople, protocol stewards, and independent developers who built the open web and the modern Android ecosystem. We build on the commons they shared, and we honor their spirit.

### On Our License

It might feel counterintuitive for us to champion open source while releasing boxlore under the [PolyForm Strict License](https://polyformproject.org/licenses/strict/1.0.0). 

We keep the entire codebase source-available because we believe in absolute transparency: you should be able to inspect every line of code, verify that our privacy promises are real, learn from how it's built, and run it for your own personal use. 

Our only restriction is **redistribution**.

The Android ecosystem is flooded with bad actors who scrape open repositories, stuff clean software with predatory ads and spyware, and re-upload them to app stores to profit off other people's labor. We fund boxlore out of our own pockets to keep it quiet, private, and ad-free. Restricting redistribution protects that mission—it guarantees that no one can take our work, strip out its soul, and turn it into the very kind of cash-grab obstacle course we built boxlore to fight against.

---

Software that helps people learn, listen, and explore should simply exist without a catch.

A true tool respects your autonomy. It doesn't clamor for your attention, gamify your curiosity, or manipulate your habits. It gives you the capability, steps quietly out of the way, and lets you listen on your own terms.
